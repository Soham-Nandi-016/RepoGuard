# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in RepoGuard a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards
- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and code conventions.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the open-source community.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting `conduct@repoguard.internal`.
